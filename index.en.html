Knowledge Base

FAQ.

Is it really free?＋

Yes. Bookmarks, highlighter, records, reminders, summaries, and exports — every feature is free. No payment method, no usage limits.

Where is my data stored?＋

Bookmarks, highlights, and records all live only in your browser's local storage. By default, AI summaries are processed on-device too — your selected text never leaves your machine.

Do highlights survive a revisit?＋

Yes. Reopen the same page and your highlights reappear in the same spots, notes included. You can browse or delete them by article in the side panel.

What's the difference between bookmarks and records?＋

Bookmarks save a page (and any selected text) as-is — a read-it-later. Records is the timeline where AI summary results stack up automatically. Together with Highlights, they make up the side panel's three tabs.

I want a smarter AI.＋

Enter your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini API key on the options page to switch to that model. Even then, your bookmarks, highlights, and records stay in your browser only.
