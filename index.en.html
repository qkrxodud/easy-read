Three things at the core — Bookmarks · Highlighter · History

Bookmarks — save it for later

Save pages to revisit as-is, no summary — with your selected sentences included. One toolbar button or a single right-click, so your reading flow never breaks.

Highlighter — stays right there

Mark key passages and attach notes. Saved highlights reappear in the same spot when you revisit the page, and they're gathered by article in the side panel.

History — builds itself

Every summary you run lands in a date-grouped timeline automatically — no saving required. With site favicons and search, "that article from last week" is one query away.

Make everything you've saved more useful

Organize with folders + tags

Attach one 📁 folder and any number of #tags to bookmarks, history entries and highlights. Combine folders, tags and search to pull out exactly what you need.

Reading reminders

So "I'll read it later" doesn't become never — set a reading time on any saved item. When it's due you get a notification and an on-page card, with daily/weekly repeats and 10-minute snooze.

Export to your notes app

Download your bookmarks, history and highlights as Markdown or copy them to the clipboard and paste straight into Notion or Obsidian. What you see filtered is what gets exported.

Search that reads the full text

Bookmarks store a text snapshot of the page, so you can find things by content even when the title escapes you. With typo tolerance, and matched sentences shown as highlighted excerpts.

And when you need it — AI that helps you read

S

Select a long read and get one conclusion sentence + key points. No selection? One right-click summarizes the whole page. Results save to history automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?
Yes. Bookmarks, the highlighter, history, reminders, summaries and export — every feature is free. No payment method to register, no usage limits.
Where is my data stored?
Bookmarks, highlights and history all live only in your computer's browser storage. With the default settings, AI summaries are processed on your device too — selected text is never sent anywhere. You search and delete everything yourself.
Do highlights survive when I revisit a page?
Yes. Reopen the same page and your highlights are redrawn in the same spot. Notes stay attached, and the side panel gathers them by article where you can browse or delete them.
What's the difference between bookmarks and history?
Bookmarks save a page (and any selected sentences) as-is, no summary — your "read it later" list. History is the timeline that fills up automatically every time you run an AI summary. Together with Highlights (highlighter + notes), they make up the side panel's three tabs.
Can I move what I've saved into my notes app?
Yes. In every tab you can download a Markdown file or copy to the clipboard, then paste into Notion, Obsidian and the like. If you've filtered by search, folder or tag, exactly what's shown is what gets exported.
What AI features are there?
Select text for a conclusion-first summary (or right-click with nothing selected to summarize the whole page), and use Write · Proofread · Tone · Translate · Ask from the right-click menu. There's also double-click word definitions and read-aloud for results. Your favorite modes can be added to the toolbar in the options.
I want a smarter AI.
Enter your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini API key on the options page to switch to that model. In that case selected text is sent to that provider — clearly explained, and entirely opt-in. Bookmarks, highlights and history still stay in your browser.
